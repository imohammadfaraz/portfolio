How I work

Understand. Build. Measure. Learn.

I like to move from ambiguity to evidence quickly. I spend enough time understanding the problem, then build something useful and learn from what happens.

01 · Understand

02 · Frame

Evidence, experiments, outcomes.

05 · Learn

What changed? What should we do next?
